01
Live — Google Play AI Mobile App

Facethetic AI

Designed, built, deployed, and monetized a production Android AI application.

Problem

Users wanted objective, data-driven feedback on facial aesthetics without the bias and cost of subjective opinions.

Solution

  • On-device facial landmark detection via MediaPipe Face Mesh — no photos sent to servers
  • Multi-factor scoring algorithm across facial symmetry, proportions, and structure
  • Personalized improvement suggestions based on analysis results
  • Score tracking over time with trend visualization

Business Model Freemium + subscription + ads
